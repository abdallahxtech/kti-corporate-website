Applies to the corporate website at kti.ae. Last prepared: 26 October 2026. KTI should review this notice with its legal/privacy adviser before publication.

The enquiry form on this website prepares a message in your own email application. The static site itself does not submit your enquiry to a website server or store a database copy. Your information will be handled by your email service and by KTI if you send the email.

Information sent to info@kti.ae may be used by KTI to respond to your enquiry, review service requirements, prepare a quotation and handle legitimate related business correspondence.

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts and contains links to Google Maps and the separately operated Starlink Odoo portal. Opening those services makes you subject to their respective privacy practices.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website package does not include analytics trackers, advertising tags or consent cookies. If additional services are added, update this notice and implement appropriate notice/consent controls where required.

5. Secure portals

Access to employee, client and inspector accounts is handled separately at starlink.kti.ae. Its authentication, data processing and access controls are administered through Odoo by KTI.
