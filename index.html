01 WHO WE ARE

Independent thinking.

Korean Techno Inspection Middle East Co. LLC (KTI) is an Abu Dhabi-based technical inspection and testing company serving the needs of industrial, energy and engineering projects.

From inspection planning and manufacturing surveillance to non-destructive examination and final technical reporting, KTI supports quality-focused decisions throughout the asset lifecycle.
